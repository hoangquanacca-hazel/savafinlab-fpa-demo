Case 1 · FMCG manufacturer (fictional “AVF”)

CFO FP&A Suite — plan, cash, capital and the board pack in one workbook

3-statementAOPZBBScenarios13-week cashROIC / EPBoard pack

The problem

A mid-size snacks, beverages and culinary producer selling through three channels needed one model that takes the plan from revenue drivers all the way to cash, value creation and a decision for the Board — instead of disconnected spreadsheets.

What the model does

  • Driver-based AOP (volume × ASP × 9 product-channel combos) and zero-based opex packages
  • Base / Upside / Downside switch flowing through the whole P&L
  • Integrated P&L → Balance Sheet → Cash Flow over 4 years; balance check = 0 every year
  • Working capital & CCC, ROIC vs WACC, Economic Profit, 13-week direct cash forecast
  • Contribution margin, portfolio matrix (Grow / Hold / Selective / Exit), mix and price-elasticity levers
  • Board pack with commentary generated from the numbers — it rewrites itself when inputs change

Case 2 · Dairy producer (fictional “DairyNova”)

Rolling forecast with budget-vs-actual tracking across two fiscal years

Rolling forecastRevenue buildBudget vs ActualCash flowScenarios

The problem

Management wanted to roll the forecast forward every month — 12 months of actuals plus 12 months ahead — and see at a glance where the business is drifting from budget, without rebuilding the file each close.

What the model does

  • Revenue built from volume × price for four product lines, with monthly growth drivers
  • Pick the cut-off month and scenario; the forecast and cash flow re-roll automatically
  • Budget FY2026 and FY2027 with line-by-line variance, favourable/unfavourable flags and status
  • Cash flow with DSO / DIO / DPO working-capital drivers, capex and tax

Case 3 · Management reporting

Price / Volume / Mix bridge with board-ready commentary

PVM analysisVariance commentaryQC checklist

The problem

Revenue came in below plan. The Board needed to know how much was price, how much was volume and how much was mix — and the explanation had to reconcile exactly, not be a narrative guess.

What the model does

  • 3-way decomposition by product and channel that reconciles exactly (difference = 0)
  • What / So-what / Now-what commentary template, with causes labelled as hypotheses until verified
  • 5-point QC checklist before anything goes to the Board
